 The n_atom means how many atoms (usually just 1 or 2(bond)) determin this center
 w (or w1,w2) is the weight from the n_atom to determine the center atom. 
 Z is the nuclear charge of this center. 
 num_G: how many  charge shells used to describe the real space charge density
       These charge shells are defined as cos funcitons on the grid, but the first grid might be special
 dmax: the maximum radius (A) of the charge ring
 dmin: a minimum radius used to determine the grid size. 
       These are the grid size used to determine the cos funciton for the basis set. 
       It has an small exponential feature for grid sizes. 
 Z: the total number of electron of the molecule. 
 factmin: a factor used to generate the grid (all the grid shift by factmin*dmin) 
 Please check the funcr_basis for the first ten basis functions.
